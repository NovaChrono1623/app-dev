# 🏁 The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift

*My favorite movie from the Fast & Furious franchise.*

---

## About the Movie

**Tokyo Drift** (2006) follows Sean Boswell, a high school street racer who gets sent to live with his father in Tokyo after a race ends in trouble. There he discovers the underground world of **drift racing** and has to learn a completely new style of driving to earn respect.

## Main Characters

- **Sean Boswell** – the new kid learning to drift
- **Han** – the laid-back mentor
- **Twinkie** – Sean's first friend in Tokyo
- **Neela** – the girl caught between two worlds
- **DK (Drift King)** – the rival Sean has to beat

## Why I Love It

1. The **drifting scenes** are unlike any other racing movie.
2. The **Tokyo setting** looks amazing, especially the night scenes.
3. The **soundtrack** fits the action perfectly.
4. Han's story makes the whole franchise more interesting.

## What Is Drifting?

> Drifting is a driving technique where the driver intentionally oversteers, causing the car to slide sideways through a corner while keeping control.
